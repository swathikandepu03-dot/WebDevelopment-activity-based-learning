Sample Travel Itineraries

Plan your journey with our suggested day-by-day schedules.

🌿 Ooty – 3 Days

Day 1: Arrival and Sightseeing Arrive in Ooty, check into your hotel, visit Ooty Lake and enjoy a relaxing evening.
Day 2: Hills and Gardens Explore the Botanical Garden, Doddabetta Peak and scenic tea gardens.
Day 3: Local Exploration Enjoy local shopping, take photographs and depart after breakfast.

🏔️ Darjeeling – 4 Days

Day 1: Arrival Arrive in Darjeeling, check into your hotel and explore the local market.
Day 2: Sunrise and Sightseeing Visit Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery.
Day 3: Tea Gardens Explore tea estates and enjoy scenic Himalayan viewpoints.
Day 4: Departure Enjoy breakfast, visit a local attraction if time permits, and depart.

🌳 Meghalaya – 5 Days

Day 1: Shillong Arrive in Shillong, check into your hotel and explore the city.
Day 2: Cherrapunji Visit waterfalls, caves and scenic viewpoints around Cherrapunji.
Day 3: Living Root Bridges Explore a living root bridge, subject to route conditions and walking requirements.
Day 4: Dawki and Mawlynnong Discover the Dawki river area and Mawlynnong village.
Day 5: Departure Enjoy breakfast and return according to your onward travel arrangements.

🏛️ Agra – 2 Days

Day 1: Taj Mahal and Agra Fort Visit the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort. Explore local markets in the evening.
Day 2: Heritage and Departure Visit another local attraction if time permits, then depart after breakfast.
